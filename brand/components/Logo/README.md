The Weaver lockup: the mark, the name and the descriptor, in three settings. A static rendition, hand-written from `src/components/site/header.tsx` (`Logo`).

**When to use:** the header and footer of every Weaver surface, the sign-in page and anything that introduces the product. Inside an organization's space the organization's own name and logo lead; Weaver appears small, in the footer.

**What the consumer provides:** the setting (`default` on `canvas` or `white`, `onAccent` on the `saffron` header, `light` on `navy`). The mark is `logos/weaver-mark.svg`; the name is always "Weaver" and the descriptor always "Community management platform".

| Setting | Mark | Name | Descriptor |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| default | 44px, on the ground | `wordmark` in `navy` | `descriptor` in `muted` |
| onAccent (header) | 44px, on `saffron` | `wordmark` in `navy` | `descriptor` in `ink` |
| light (footer) | 32px on a 44px `white` tile, `radius-logo-tile` | `wordmark` in `white` | `descriptor` in `navy-200` |

Do: keep 6px between the mark and the name. Don't: put the bare mark on `navy`, recolour it, or set the name in any face but Bricolage Grotesque.
