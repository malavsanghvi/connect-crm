HOW A PRODUCT IS SWITCHED

States, who changes them, and what happens to data

Only Weaver turns a product on or off A platform admin, with a reason and a fresh code from an authenticator app, every time. Each change is in the audit log.
The community pauses and resumes Its admins request a product and can pause or resume one that is on. They cannot turn on what Weaver has not.
No switch deletes anything Off, paused or lapsed, the data is kept. Installed member apps follow the switch with no app update.
