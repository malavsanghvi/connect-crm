How people receive it

  1. A notification on their phone: "Amira sent you a video message".
  2. Tapping it opens the app straight to the video.
  3. It plays in the app with captions. There is no download, save or share button.
  4. No app? An email with a link that opens the same player after they sign in.

What "not downloadable" means

The app streams the video and never offers the file. The link behind it works only for the person signed in and expires within minutes. Someone could still film their screen; the app just never hands over the file.
