Moments that matter

1. Nobody knowsStage 1 loses the most matches. A gentle, general ask beats any later fix.
2. Excluded, found lateMany programs exclude religious organizations. Saying so at stage 3 avoids a disappointed donor at stage 7.
3. The portal stepStage 7 is the biggest drop-off: deep links and the exact registered name matter most.
4. DeadlinesA missed deadline is money lost for good. Reminders and the filing queue both work against it.
5. Trust in the feeSuccess only, capped, invoiced separately, every match listed. Never taken out of the gift.
