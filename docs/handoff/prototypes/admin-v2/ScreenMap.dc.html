The review of every admin screen

From 138 screens to 11 workspaces

Six reviewers read every page in the portal. This is what they found, and where each area goes. The pages are grouped by the job people do, not by the table the data sits in.

Six things we found almost everywhere
Organised by data, not by job. Recording one payment can cross four pages. Getting texting working crosses five.
Long forms and half-finished saves. The event form has about 35 fields and saves any you left out as blank. Some saves report “other changes were saved” in a sentence.
Consequences hidden until after. Marking an event completed opens a survey and queues notices with no confirmation. Cancelling an event tells nobody.
Households shown by name alone in at least eight places, against our own rule.
Dead ends. Buttons that are permanently off, and settings that are only recorded and do nothing, such as session length.
Rules kept in screen code, not in the database. Anything that skips the screen, such as an assistant, would skip the rule too.
