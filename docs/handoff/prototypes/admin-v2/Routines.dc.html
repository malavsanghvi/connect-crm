How a routine works
1It meets you at sign-in. Today shows your routine first, with what is due today.
2Each step goes to the exact place, or Niva walks through it with you, one step at a time.
3Steps tick themselves when the work is really done, such as an empty approval queue. The rest you tick.
4Nothing is lost. A step you skip comes back at your next sign-in. Weekly and month-end steps appear on their day.
5You only see what you may do. If you hold two roles, the routines are joined and grouped by role.
A routine never acts on its own. Niva drafts and prepares. Anything that moves money, messages members, changes a role or deletes something waits for your confirmation.
