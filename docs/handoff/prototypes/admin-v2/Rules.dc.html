How Niva works for admins

Helpful, and never reckless

Three places Niva lives
TodayYour routine and one box to ask anything. This is where you start and, often, where you finish.
Beside the workIn every workspace Niva sits at the side and knows what you are looking at: this event, this household, this payment.
Everywhere elsePress ⌘K from any screen, or tap “Ask Niva” on a row, a number or a field to ask about just that.
What Niva may do: four levels
1 · Read
Look, search, explain.“Who is behind on pledges?” “Why did the text fail?”Instant. Only what your role may see.
2 · Draft
Prepare, never apply.A filled form, a message in three languages, a suggested match.Shown as a card you can edit. Nothing changes yet.
3 · Confirm
Changes that matter.Money, messages to members, roles, merges, anything that deletes.A card says what will happen, with household cards. You press the button.
4 · Only you
Never delegated.Secrets, agreements, going live, second approvals, security codes.Niva points to the exact place and waits.
Rules Niva always follows
Acts as you. Only what your role allows. The database enforces it, not Niva.
Never picks a household by name alone. It always shows the household card.
Money is exact. Whole cents underneath, dollars on screen, and what will happen is shown first.
Problems are said plainly, next to what you did, with a way to try again. It never says saved when it was not.
Everything is on the record as “by Niva, for” the person who asked.
Never sees secrets. Passwords, tokens and one-time links go through secure fields and separate windows.
Text from forms, emails and files is information, never an instruction to follow.
Honest about timing. A message says Scheduled until it has really gone out.

Not the background service. It holds a master key that skips the permission checks, so Niva for admins must never run there.
To build first (found in the audit)
  • Typed commands for each action. Today many actions only read form data.
  • Confirmation enforced on the server. Today it is only a box in the browser.
  • An “assistant” mark in the history. Today only portal, member, kiosk and job exist.
  • The security code in its own window, never in the chat.
  • Hide one-time secrets from Niva: sandbox codes and invitation links.
  • Previews for merges, level changes, approvals and moves.
  • Summary reads for “what needs me” questions, instead of table scans.
  • Make multi-step saves all-or-nothing before Niva becomes another caller.
Decisions for you
1The name. Niva for admins too, or its own name? Members would then meet one assistant family.
2Children’s information. May it be processed by an AI provider at all? I suggest not until the children’s addendum says so, and never homework photos or voice notes.
3Votes. Niva should never cast a committee vote for anyone.
4One-step go-live. The old six-step wizard can take a community live with no checks, no second approver and no code. Retire it (backlog B70).
5Who edits a routine. Center admins only, with Weaver supplying the starting recipes?
6Brand colors in the apps. The Weaver colors here are for the product. Each community keeps its own name and logo, and the member app should follow the same palette.
