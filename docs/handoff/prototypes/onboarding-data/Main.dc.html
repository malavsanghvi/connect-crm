What Niva sees, and what it never does

  • To suggest where a column goes, I see the column headings and a few sample values with names, digits and dates masked. Your full file stays in Weaver.
  • Nothing about children. Columns and lines that look like children's names, ages or schools are not shown to me at all, and photos and PDFs are read on Weaver's own servers, not by an AI service.
  • I never save anything on my own, and I never merge two families because their names look alike.
  • Keys are stored encrypted. You see the last four characters, and changing or removing one needs a fresh 2FA check.
