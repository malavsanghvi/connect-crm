Payment proofs, soft credit and bank reconciliation

A member who paid another way (cash, check, bank transfer, stock) sends a photo or file of the receipt. The pledges it covers, oldest first, show Soft credit. The treasury team verifies the proof, the pledges become Paid, and when the bank statement shows the money they become Bank reconciled.

The open-balance rule

Open balance = amount − paid − soft credit, added up over pledges that are Open or Partly paid.

A pledge in Soft credit, Paid or Bank reconciled adds nothing to it. Soft credit is shown as its own figure beside it, never inside it. This holds on every screen, for members and for the treasury team. Giving totals count only verified payments.
