# Review of the gamified Gyan Path PRs — 2026-10-01

Six independent reviewers (database security, points integrity, migration safety; app flow, voice and native modules, UI and house rules) read connect-crm PR #64 (0570, points engine) at 3f6dff4 and connect-mobile PR #50 (lessons, voice, runtime 3) at 1f641d9. A skeptical verifier per PR tried to refute every finding: **62 raised, 59 confirmed** (3 rejected). Severities below are the verifier's. **Nothing here is fixed yet** — the work was parked (BACKLOG B43). PR #66 (0571 content) is stacked on #64.

**Deferral plan agreed when parking:** fix every should_fix before merge; most notes are cheap and were planned for the same pass; crm #4, #10, #13, #14 and mobile #47 may go to the backlog; crm #21 (types to connect-admin) is the normal post-merge step.
